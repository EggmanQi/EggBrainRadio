EggBrain Radio is the English-facing home for what I build and what I learn while building it.

The structure is intentionally small:

1. **Work** — product pages with store and repo links.
2. **Words** — notes, essays, and operating journals.

No dashboard clutter on the first screen. Just the studio name, one sentence, and two doors.
