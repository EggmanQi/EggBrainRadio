---
title: Build in public, ship for overseas
summary: A short operating note on writing for App Store and extension users first.
date: 2026-09-05
tags: [ops, overseas]
---

If the product is for overseas users, the site should speak their language first.

That means:

- English as the canonical content.
- Product pages that answer “what does it do?” in one screen.
- Store links above the fold on every project page.

Domestic channels can remix later. The source of truth stays here.
